# Chapter 1: The Letter

The letter came on a grey morning in late autumn. Ada read it twice at the kitchen table--once quickly, once slowly--and then put it in the drawer with the others.

"Is it from him?" her sister asked.

"It is," said Ada. "He says he will come at the end of the month... if the roads allow."

Her sister said nothing. She had heard that before, twelve times at least, and she recognised the tone. The kettle boiled. Ada made the tea, and they drank it in the parlour without another word about the letter.

* * *

He came on the thirtieth, as he had promised, with two bags and a parcel of books. Ada met him at the gate. Neither of them spoke until they were inside.
