# Chapter 1: Market Day

The carts came in from the farms before dawn. By eight the square was full. A woman from the hill farms sold eggs from a basket; a boy beside her sold string. The church clock struck the hour and nobody looked up.

At noon the baker shut his door. The smith went on working. Two dogs fought over a bone near the pump until a carter threw water at them, and then they lay down together in the shade of his wagon.

In the afternoon the rain came. Stallholders pulled their covers over and stood under the awnings, talking about prices. By four it had stopped. The carts went home along the wet road, one by one, and by dark the square was empty again.
