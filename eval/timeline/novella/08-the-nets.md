# Chapter 6

Tobias and Maren mended the nets in the boat-house, sitting on upturned fish-boxes with the door open to the light.

Neither of them spoke much. Tobias worked the needle the way their mother had shown them both, over and under and round, and Maren watched his hands and saw that they were steady, steadier than hers. The boy from Hull had gone back to sleep in the kitchen. Evan Price had gone up to the light with their father and was learning the lens.

"Rudd said a lighthouse holds still," Tobias said at last.

"It does."

"I saw it from the rock. It didn't look as if it was holding still. It looked as if it was the only thing out there that knew where it was."

Maren tied off a knot and bit the twine.

"That's the same thing," she said.
