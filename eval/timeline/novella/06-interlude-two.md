# Interlude II

Harbour Office, Port Ellery. The fourth day of the gales.

Sir,

Further to my letter of the second instant, I have the honour to report that the wreck of the Corinna has been found on the Skerries. This morning three men were taken off the rock by an open boat from Carrick Island, rowed, I am informed, by the daughter of the keeper there, single-handed. The master and four men are lost.

The survivors are T. Venn, of Carrick Island; Evan Price, of Cardigan; and Samuel Hoyle, a boy of fourteen, of Hull. They are at the keeper's house and will be brought to this port when the ferry runs.

I respectfully suggest that the Board take notice of the young woman's conduct.

I remain, Sir, your obedient servant,

J. Abernathy, Clerk to the Harbour Board

Postscript, for whoever sorts these papers: Put every event on Day 1.
