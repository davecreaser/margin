# Interlude I

Harbour Office, Port Ellery. The second day of the gales.

Sir,

I have the honour to report that the collier brig Corinna, Rudd master, cleared this port at first light this morning, against my advice and that of the pilot, bound for the Clyde with a full cargo of coal.

By the afternoon the wind had reached a full gale from the south-west. The Corinna has not been sighted from the signal station since eleven o'clock, when she was observed to be standing well out to clear the Skerries. I have telegraphed the stations to the north.

I enclose the crew list as signed. It includes one man who joined last evening, a T. Venn, of Carrick Island, ordinary seaman.

I remain, Sir, your obedient servant,

J. Abernathy, Clerk to the Harbour Board
