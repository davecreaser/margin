# Interlude

Wendle Vicarage, the fifth of March.

My dear Lettice,

You will have read in the paper that Hollin Bridge is down. We heard it from the Vicarage on the night of the third, a little before eleven, a dreadful groaning sound, and Silas went out in his nightshirt to look, which you may imagine.

The inquest sat today at the Fleece, on the poor man who was drowned. Silas knew him, a Mr Lusk, a cattle dealer. Accidental death, of course. Mrs Garrow at the mill sat at the back in her black and never once looked up.

I do not like that woman's face this week, Lettice. I cannot tell you why.

Your loving sister,

Hester Thorne
