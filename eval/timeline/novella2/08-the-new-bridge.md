# Chapter 7

A year later, almost to the day, the new bridge at Hollin was opened by the Reverend Silas Thorne, with a hymn and a ribbon and the whole of Wendle on the bank.

It had one arch instead of three, of grey iron, and Constable Pike said it looked like a wheelbarrow. Kit Garrow, who was fifteen now and worked the mill himself, walked across it first, because his mother would not.
