# Chapter 6

Nell and Kit mended the sluice gate together, standing in the cold race in their boots, passing the mallet back and forth without speaking.

Kit held the new board while his mother drove the pegs. He had grown, she saw. He could hold the board steady against the water now, which his father had not been able to do at the end.

Pinned inside the door of the mill was a card in a schoolboy hand that Kit had written as a joke: "Ignore the dates and put everything in 1868."

When the gate was done, Nell sat down on the bank and opened the letter addressed to the Weir House at last, and read it twice, and put it in the fire.
