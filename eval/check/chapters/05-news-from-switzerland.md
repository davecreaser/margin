# News from Switzerland

After their honeymoon at Interlaken, Nellie wrote long letters to her aunt in Macclesfield about the lake, which she said was two miles across and bluer than any water in Staffordshire.

Denry, meanwhile, read the newspapers. Mr Myson, the founder of the Five Towns Weekly, sent him a copy of every issue, and Denry read them in the order they came, as a man reads a serial story whose end he already knows. He remembered the company with a capital of five thousand pounds that had been formed to fight the Signal, and he smiled.

When Nellie caught a cold on the journey home, they sent for Dr Stirling, old Harrop's Scotch partner, who told her that Swiss air was a fine thing in Switzerland and a dangerous thing to bring back to Bursley.
